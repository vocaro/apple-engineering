# Measurement

These mechanisms and lessons apply when the stated capability is present. Original
measurements establish their recorded platform and workload; they do not establish
universal behavior on later operating systems or in a different application.

## Performance and memory measurement

Fresh-child peak RSS, footprint corroboration, host-pressure records, CPU versus scheduling time, release builds, repeated-process checks and bounded reruns.

[PDFReflowLib doc/memory-testing.md](https://github.com/vocaro/PDFReflowLib/blob/4a625316a5b4a4aafea5134dfcfec1a6991e9cbd/doc/memory-testing.md) and [PDFReflowLib doc/regression-testing.md](https://github.com/vocaro/PDFReflowLib/blob/4a625316a5b4a4aafea5134dfcfec1a6991e9cbd/doc/regression-testing.md#tests-that-pin-a-cost). Budgets stay local and platform-specific; desktop RSS is not a mobile shipping budget.
