# Framework execution identity

These mechanisms and lessons apply when the stated capability is present. Original
measurements establish their recorded platform and workload; they do not establish
universal behavior on later operating systems or in a different application.

## Executable identity and execution environment can alter OCR

Identical converter bytes under another basename produced different recognized text on the measured Mac. A crossed binary/environment experiment also traced apparent output regressions to sandbox capabilities rather than source revisions. Control basename, signing, environment and capability evidence when comparing Vision results; directory presence alone does not establish a warm or identical compile.
