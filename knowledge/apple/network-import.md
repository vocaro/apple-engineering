# Network import

These mechanisms and lessons apply when the stated capability is present. Original
measurements establish their recorded platform and workload; they do not establish
universal behavior on later operating systems or in a different application.

## Network import policy must survive redirects and decompression

Re-enter scope, robots and private-network checks on every redirect hop rather than letting URLSession follow outside the policy layer. Bound post-decompression bytes while streaming. URL, body and extracted-text identities detect different duplicate/flood patterns.

[studywright doc/components/ingestion.md](https://github.com/vocaro/studywright/blob/f25d97a147e63c8c9a3bdbebaad2ea1c7e957e8c/doc/components/ingestion.md). These mechanisms are reusable for network importers; corpus-specific permissions and authenticated-source behavior remain independently governed.
