# Inference and evidence

These mechanisms and lessons apply when the stated capability is present. Original
measurements establish their recorded platform and workload; they do not establish
universal behavior on later operating systems or in a different application.

## Inference availability must be tested through the real execution path

Record why an entitled signed app, a command-line process, tests and a simulator can reach different capabilities. Preserve typed errors before an NSError bridge loses payloads; distinguish empty generation, rate limiting, quota refusal, safety filtering and context overflow. A successful control must use the intended route.

## Retrieval, attribution and truth are different checks

A retrieved passage may be relevant yet insufficient; faithful citation of an outdated source is not factual correctness. Conversation history admitted as evidence can validate an earlier fabricated answer by construction. Preserve failed approaches to similarity thresholds, self-judging and context truncation, and distinguish structural validity from semantic qualification.
