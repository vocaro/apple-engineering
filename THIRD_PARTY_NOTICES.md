# Source provenance

EPUBLib VoiceOver mechanisms and native helpers originate in vocaro/EPUBLib at
1f164cb8239a1573639c32f5df0e397a4385753a under its MIT licence. The original qualification
records and their platform limits remain in that repository. Its licence is retained beside
the extracted source in licenses/epublib-mit.txt.

Pagepour's evidence, capture and offscreen hosting mechanisms and PDFReflowLib's coordination
mechanisms originate in private proprietary repositories owned by Vocaro. The extracted
reusable mechanisms in this repository are released under its MIT licence by Vocaro; this
does not change the licences or visibility of the original projects. Their source references
are in the knowledge index. No external Python runtime dependency is required.

MathMLLayout's exact-manifest report validator originates in vocaro/MathMLLayout at
8e038411260803c3b272dff590129516e7d846d7 under MIT; its licence is retained in
licenses/mathmllayout-mit.txt. StudyWright's native-Python preflight originates in the private
studywright repository at f25d97a147e63c8c9a3bdbebaad2ea1c7e957e8c. The extracted preflight is also released under this repository's MIT licence; its source references
and app-specific environment repair policy remain with that project.
