# Simulator pool

Use only the approved pool, and take a shut-down device under a real reservation; a booted one
is another session's. Erase only your claimed device (it keeps its UDID and approvals), boot it
to `bootstatus -b`, pass its canonical uppercase UDID, copy evidence off, then shut it down. Do
not create, rename or delete pool simulators without owner authorization. Do not open a live
simulator view unless the owner asked to watch. Set Duo poses by automation and confirm the
observed display; never use screenConfig on the Duo simulator. Pass test environment with
`TEST_RUNNER_` and require a positive executed count. Agree a window with the owner before any
session on the paired iPhone or iPad, and still reserve it. Methods:
guides/simulator-testing.md, guides/device-testing.md.
