# Background processing

Submission, acceptance and a runtime grant differ. Submit from the person's foreground action,
off the main actor, under a fresh per-attempt identifier; bound the grant wait, reject late
grants and scope expiration to its own attempt. Move a task's count only on evidence of work.
Inject measured policy intervals and preserve actual device qualification; a foreground probe
does not qualify background compute. BackgroundTaskLib owns mechanisms; apps own admission,
entitlements and persistence.
